Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect personal information.

Who we are

Trivio Rescue operates this website at https://www.triviorescue.com/.

Information we collect

When you use our contact form, we collect the information you provide, such as your name, email address, and message. We may also receive limited technical information from your browser or device, such as your IP address, browser type, pages visited, and the time of your visit.

How we use your information

We use your information to respond to enquiries, provide or improve our services, manage customer relationships, maintain the security of our website, and meet legal or regulatory obligations.

Cookies

Our website may use essential cookies or similar technologies required for the site to function properly. These do not identify you personally. You can manage cookies through your browser settings.

Forms and service providers

Contact form submissions are processed through Formspree, our form-processing provider. Formspree may process the information you submit in accordance with its own privacy policy. We only use submitted information to respond to your enquiry and provide relevant services.

Who we share your data with

We do not sell customer information. We may share information with trusted service providers only when necessary to operate our website, process enquiries, provide our services, or comply with applicable law.

How long we retain your data

We retain enquiry and service information only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to you, provide services, maintain business records, or meet legal, tax, and security obligations.

What rights you have over your data

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold about you, subject to any legal obligations that require us to retain it. To make a request, please contact us using the details below.

Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how Trivio Rescue handles your information, please contact us at contact@triviorescue.com.
