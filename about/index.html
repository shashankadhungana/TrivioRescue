Who We Are

Trivio Rescue — your Australian technology lifecycle partner

We specialise in repairs, procurement, asset management, and responsible e-waste recycling for organisations across Australia.

Our Story

Trivio Rescue was founded to fill a market gap: too many organisations default to replacing devices rather than assessing whether they can be repaired or reused. We deliver honest audits and practical pathways to extend device life and recover value.

Vision

To be the trusted partner Australian organisations rely on to manage the full lifecycle of their technology — reducing costs, extending value, and minimising risk at every stage.

Mission

We operate as an extension of our clients' IT teams, solving technology challenges quickly and effectively while prioritising cost, uptime, and sustainability.
