Our Story

Trivio Rescue was founded to challenge the traditional approach to technology lifecycle management.

Too often, organisations replace devices without first understanding whether they can be repaired, refurbished, redeployed, or have their value recovered. This can lead to unnecessary expenditure, avoidable downtime, and electronic waste.

We believe there is a better approach.

Trivio Rescue helps Australian organisations make smarter decisions about the technology they already own. Our approach is built around honest assessment, practical advice, and commercially responsible outcomes — helping clients extend device life cycles, reduce unnecessary costs, and make informed decisions about when to repair, reuse, recover, or replace.

We work alongside our clients as an extension of their IT teams, combining technical expertise with a practical understanding of business requirements. Our focus is not simply on fixing or supplying technology, but on helping organisations maximise the value of their technology assets throughout their life cycle.

Our Foundation

Backed by My Phone Rescue

Trivio Rescue is a registered trading name of My Phone Rescue, our parent company, which has built a strong reputation in technology repair, diagnostics, and device life cycle management since 2018. Operating under the My Phone Rescue ABN, Trivio Rescue extends this expertise into the business and enterprise sector, providing organisations with strategic technology life cycle services, asset audits, and value recovery solutions.

Together, the two brands combine extensive technical expertise with a shared commitment to helping customers get more from their technology through smarter decisions, exceptional service, and sustainable outcomes.
