How We Work

Four-stage process

Assess → Recommend → Deliver → Recover & Report

Device Repair & Maintenance

Certified repairs for laptops, phones, tablets and motherboards. Same Day turnaround for most mobile phones; 1 Week typical for full device repairs depending on parts availability.

Technology Procurement

Sourcing new and refurbished technology at competitive prices with full procurement support. Typical sourcing turnaround: 2 Weeks Max.

Asset Lifecycle Management

End-to-end lifecycle management from deployment, tracking, maintenance and end-of-life planning, reducing total cost of ownership.

Sustainability & Recovery

Our methodology: Assess → Recover → Refurb → Recycle. We prioritise reuse and refurbishment before recycling to maximise recovery value and reduce e-waste.

Data Security & Confidentiality

We handle devices with strict security controls, restricted access, and secure data-wiping practices. Clients must back up their data before handing over devices; we are not responsible for unrecoverable personal data if backups are not provided.
